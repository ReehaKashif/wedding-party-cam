WEDDING PARTY CAM — AUTO CAMERA + PHOTO AND VIDEO

1. In your GitHub Pages repository, replace the existing index.html with this one.
2. Keep the Custom domain field empty in Settings > Pages.
3. If you have NOT yet run video_setup.sql, paste it into Supabase SQL Editor and click Run.
4. Open the GitHub Pages website directly in Safari on iPhone. Allow camera and microphone permissions when prompted.
5. The camera should open automatically. If Safari requires interaction, a small Enable Camera button appears.
6. Tap PHOTO or VIDEO to choose mode. In VIDEO, tap the shutter once to record and again to stop; clips are limited to 30 seconds and 20 MB.
7. Open Gallery to view shared uploads.

The Supabase URL and publishable key are included in index.html. Never add secret or service_role keys to public GitHub files.

Free-tier storage and bandwidth are limited. Public anonymous uploads need rate limiting, moderation and abuse protection before wide public sharing.
