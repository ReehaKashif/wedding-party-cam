Wedding Party Cam — photo + video update

1. In Supabase SQL Editor run video_setup.sql (your existing photo setup remains).
2. Replace your GitHub Pages index.html with this updated index.html and commit.
3. Wait for GitHub Pages to redeploy; reload Safari.
4. Tap VIDEO, allow microphone access, tap shutter to record, tap again to stop.
5. Video uploads automatically and appears in the gallery.

Video maximum: 30 seconds or 20 MB, whichever is reached first.
Free Supabase storage/bandwidth quotas apply. Browser recording formats vary: some browsers may record WebM, which may not play on every iPhone. Test on your guests' devices.
Public no-login uploads have spam/abuse risk; do not share publicly until protections are added.
